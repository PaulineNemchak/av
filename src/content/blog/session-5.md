The party began their investigation of the gauntlight lighthouse. The party immediately encountered a Haunt in the tower base in the form of a column of blood in the shape of a human woman, who Genevive identified as Belcorra. The blood was seeping into the room from below. Calling upon the power of her goddess, Laurah was able to drive the spirit away, and it fled through an iron trapdoor in the ceiling. The trapdoor was sealed with a complex and robust lock, but the party managed to open it.

Emerging into the room containing gauntlight's light, Quintus detected the obvious signs of powerful necromantic magic in the sickly blue glow emitted from the central light. This eldritch force quickly boiled away the spray of blood from the haunt, animating it into a Vampiric Mist. It was defeated with fire and needle darts. Lilith decided to retire after this encounter.

The party proceeded to investigate the gauntlight in line with Wrin's request. Gauntlight light was emitting powerful necromantic magic so strong that it had spontaneously formed a Vampiric mist in seconds. That the source of the Haunt on the lower level was magical in nature and originated in a lower level of the keep. That the Haunt would recur until this magical process was ended.

The party proceeded to eliminate the untamed Giant Scorpion. Healing up following the combat, they beheld a stone door, which Laurah recognizad as emblazoned with the symbol of the Outer God Nhimbaloth.

> Nhimbaloth is a shapeless entity known as the Empty Death. She preys on those who hunt souls as they travel down the river, but she devours both hunter and soul indiscriminately; those she consumes have no afterlife or potential for resurrection. They are forever gone, and forever nothing.

The party entered the chapel to find a skeleton clawing at a hidden door, slain by two Corpselights that emerged from the transept animating old remains. Searching the first skeleton following the combat revealed that he had clearly been a ruin delver of some sort, carrying adventurer's gear including an enchanted rapier, while the corpselight skeletons had valuable gold teeth and an enchanted amulet.

Proceeding through the hidden door revealed a private sanctum in a small triangular room, searching it revealed a set of keys, many of which had rusted away. The remaining keys were two ornate brass articles, tarnished with age, emblazoned with icons of a lighthouse and a book respectively. The party surmised that the former was the key to the trapdoor they opened in the gauntlight, but there was no sign of a library on this level.

The hidden private sanctum opened out into an office adjoining the central hallway. Searching it revealed a hidden door in the east wall, with a rotating shelf on the wall serving as the door's handle. Behind that door was a perfectly preserved and hitherto undiscovered stairwell, leading down into blackness. A desk in the office contained some ancient but still potent alchemical elixirs.

Having investigated the Gauntlight and revealed a path below, the party determined to retire to Otari as the first rays of dawn began to lance over the horizon. Gauntlight Keep is clearly not just a fortress built by Belcorra, but merely the upper level of some deeper complex, with potent necromantic energies emerging from below. Wrin Sivinxi will be eager to hear the party's report, Tamily Tanderveil may be able to provide workmen to clear the accessway the Mitflits used to flee the "Mushroom eyed men", while the lead on Yinyasmera's men and the "Swamp Dragon" is as yet unexplored, and the boathouse and small island off to the north remain unknown to the party.
