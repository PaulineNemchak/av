The group encountered an ally exploring the ruins, Genevive, who was immediately identified by Lilith as a former member of the Absalom city watch. The central hall had a secret room with the remains of a teleportation circle, damaged beyond repair.

We've met the mitflits' leader, Boss Skrawng. He was planning an invasion. Bonk then revealed that the party's investigation of gauntlight would require the mitflits and their gathered army of vermin leave. This enraged Skrawng and he began a doomed attack on the party. The mitflits put up little resistance, and after killing Skrawng's pet giant solifugid and pinning him to the wall with Lilith's rapier, the party coerced him into clearing the remaining vermin from the southern part of the gauntlight and leaving. Skrawng refused to attempt to deal with the untamed giant scorpion to the northwest though - "more'n my life's worth".

Quintus noted that the party could rest and make preparations now, and investigate the Gauntlight at night with it's haunts at their most active. Nightfall revealed that the Gauntlight was indeed lit, and the whole party experienced the unquiet spirits of this place. First investigating the dining room, they were set upon by a haunt of long-dead kobold warriors, who set the party against one another. Faced with an enemy that could not be fought head on, it took a combination of Lauh-Rah's religious studies and Genevive's anthropological knowledge to uncover how to set these souls at rest.

The Gauntlight keep has been confirmed to have an additional level, albeit with it's only current entrance blocked with debris that would take a team of workmen a day to clear.
