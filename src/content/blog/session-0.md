### Description
Fate has brought adventurers to the Crow's Casks tavern, where local notable Tamily Tanderveil has called an impromptu town meeting to address rumours of problems at the Tanderveil family’s fishery. Some are saying that some sort of beast is lurking in the basement, feeding on the stores of salted fish. Folks are worried that whatever is eating the fish might get hungry enough to eat the fishermen next! With the town guard busy protecting the loggers, Tamily needs a few brave souls to venture down into the basement of her warehouse and discover the truth of the matter.

Adventurers fought giant rats and giant spiders in the basement. Undead rised from the tombs and were defeated. In one of the rooms there was a statue of an octopus with a bowl of water that cleanes everyting, but it didn't work outside of the room.
