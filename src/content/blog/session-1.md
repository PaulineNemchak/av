The party went to fight a dragon, and then brought it's head to Tamily. Tamily said that there's a real estate in the fishing camp we can take if we can clear it of evil creatures. A ghost lives in the house.

We've met Carman Rajani, a blacksmith in Otari, and traded with him. He is a descendant of Vol Rajani, the town's co-founder.

On our way we met a few boards and had to fight them.
