The party headed back to town for some well earned food and rest. Their plans were rudely interrupted. Whilst keenly bartering away their hard earned loot, the light from the Gauntlight narrowed into an eerie beam pointed directly at the Dawnflower library. The party ran towards the library, amid shouts that the dead had risen and were throwing themselves off the cliffs above at the library. Handing two wands of healing to a nearby acolyte and ordering them to accompany them to ward off the oncoming evil, they arrived at the foot of the tower. By the steep staircase cut into the rocks an argument broke out. Vandy Banderdash, high cleric of the Dawnflower library, was rumoured to be in the graveyard above, either dead or imprisoned by the zombie hoard. The acolytes pleaded with the town guard to launch a rescue mission rather than hold their barricade. The party as one stepped up and rushed up the steps to face the horde.

Lardus Longsaddle told Nin off for impugning the courage of his men and assuming half a squadron of part time crossbowmen could go toe to toe with a zombie horde. Quintus used the rest of his necklace of fireballs to blast a route through the zombies while the rest of the party got to grips with the undead adventurers. The acolyte used the wands of Heal the party left him to keep them up, and was pretty confused when they didn't work on Erzsebet. The party found Vandy pinned to a tree guarded by a Scalathrax and she supported them with Heal and Blazing Armoury while they fought the creature.

Politically Carman is running against Oseph, and in the panic of the attack Oseph conspired with Tamily to imply that the town of Otari hired the party to investigate the Gauntlight already. He's christened them "The Fogfen Group" and offered the party sponsorship.

The party had a Harrow reading and were awarded a harrow card:

- Good past: The Beating (misaligned - the party have recently won a victory)
- Neutral past: The Vision (Wrin’s visions?)
- Evil past: The Tyrant (Belcorra? This is a True Match, so it is particularly important)
- Good present: The Survivor (they will meet someone who has endured great evil. This is another True Match)
- Neutral present: The Hidden Truth (the party will uncover many secrets)
- Evil present: The Demon’s Lantern (will-o-wisps have been seen around the Fogfen and are associated with Nhimbaloth)
- Good future: The Locksmith (the party will open many doors which have long remained closed)
- Neutral future: The Fiend (a great and dangerous power may aid or harm the party)
- Neutral future: The Eclipse (The Eclipse in the evil future is a dire omen indeed)

There were some announcements at the Hustings:

- Brelda Venkervale at the Rockfish is still hoping for news of her missing son
- Keelo is upping the bounty on the Werewolf Jaul Mezmin to 300 gold pieces!
- Lady Inkleholtz is planning to buy the remains of the Thirsty Alpaca and renovate it into
  a school
  Tamily Tanderveil is displaying a dragon head at the Otari Fishery

Vandy described how the Gauntlight beam had teleported the Scalathrax into the graveyard to clear the way for the zombies to dig themselves out, and told you that the necromancy involved must be incredibly powerful to raise the dead so close to a temple.

Brindek was motivated by new rewards and joined the party.
