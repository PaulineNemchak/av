The party sprang into combat with the morlock engineers, with Bonk leading the charge as Nin carried the now unconscious Genevieve to safety. A fierce but brief battle ensued, with Bonk grappling with the morlocks and preventing them bringing their weapons to bear. After the first engineer was killed, the second fled, with aklo speakers noting that he was crying out attempting to warn "Graulgust", but the fleeing morlock succumbed to hemorrhage from one of Erszebet's attacks and fell without passing on his message. The group explored the workshop, noting that these were the ballistae that had been mounted on the gauntlight's rail system, and there was probably enough material here to construct a full artillery piece. continuing to explore this sector, they came across a room with a jumbled pile of adventuring gear, including modern gold coins minted in Absalom. The same room contained a well, which they reconnoitered by tying Erszebet's familiar to a rope and having her see through its eyes. This revealed a further sublevel to the complex, in the form of a wide cavern containing a Gibbering Mouther. They elected not to face this challenge for now, but noted this access point to a deeper level of the complex. Proceeding onwards, they uncovered a makeshift jail containing the owners of the adventuring gear in a pathetic state, emaciated and filthy. These were clearly Ziskelly Theed and his men, who Yinyasmera had asked the party to find.

Theed explained that they had discovered the cove under the boathouse and hoped to loot the deeper levels of the Gauntlight, but had been trapped here when Freznelkesh had returned to the cavern and blocked it behind them. They had engaged in running battles with the morlocks but been worn down and taken prisoner. One of the prisoners had been taken by the morlocks and not returned. The Party sent the rescued thieves back to the surface with Genevieve, and the rest proceeded onwards in the direction of the sounds of excavation caused by the workmen above. They emerged into a central chamber where they could see the other side of the cave in, filled with morlocks who had been drawn by the sound of digging, who were being declaimed to by a larger morlock who was obviously their leader Graulgust. Graulgist attempted to open negotiations with the party in Aklo, but Quintus was not taking any chances and deployed his Necklace of Fireballs, which in concert with the rest of the party's attacks made short work of the full population of morlocks on this floor. They then rendezvoused with the workmen upon their breaking through the other side of the rubble, dismissing them once the way to the surface was open. The party continued to explore this level, revealing a small conference room behind a hidden doorway which would serve as an excellent spot to rest, Graulgust's throne room, and a cavern containing a Grothlut fleshwarp, which they quickly dispatched. They also discovered that the kitchen on this floor had been converted into a makeshift temple to the "Ghost Queen", with iconography resembling the apparition of belcorra that the party encountered in the lighthouse above. An altar here held the sacrificed body of the fourth of Theed's men, along with a pile of offerings including magical items. A secret door behind the iconography led to a crypt adjoining this floor's section of the central tower, clearly a position of honour close to the nexus of necromantic energy.

Entering the crypt, Erzsebet immediately identified the Shadow emerging from the central sarcophagus and barked out its weaknesses and resistances, enabling the rest of the party to focus on vitality and force damage and drop it quickly, despite a brutal attack on quintus that almost slew him. Exhausted, the party determined to beat a retreat for now, and continue their explorations towards the central tower, and perhaps even of the deeper level they have uncovered, after recovering. Nin mused about using rebuilding the ballista and using it against Freznelkesh, as the loading dock they uncovered connected to the ceiling rail system, while other members of the party were conscious of the upcoming hustings in Otari, wanting to be present and have the opportunity to sell their accumulated loot.
