The party reconvened on the island north of the Gauntlight, with Nin and Quintus returning from their separate investigations in Otari. Nin brought news of Narala the centaur’s disappearance and the Immenwood Rangers’ resultant refusal to assist the Civic Guard, while Quintus shared the information he had uncovered concerning Nhimbaloth, The party were all rested and fighting fit, though they remained contractually tied to the ruins for another two days while Tamily’s workmen continued excavating the blocked route deeper into the Gauntlight complex. With the island otherwise thoroughly explored, Genevieve took up watch over the labourers, while the rest of the party resolved to investigate Volluk's workshop further.

Descending into the darkness, they found themselves in a damp, mouldy and apparently inhabited underground complex of shabby furniture, cobwebs and swollen wooden doors. Party's first investigations uncovered a small bedroom and study containing a scrap of paper listing several occult books — What the Worm Knows, Grave Feasts and Secrets of the Skull — alongside an Elven note promising to return the books to their owner once their transcription was complete. The books themselves were nowhere to be found. A neighbouring servants’ room proved more rewarding: Detect Magic revealed a loose brick concealing a small cache containing a jade shard, a silver statuette of two succubi, and a permanently oily rune stone which Quintus identified as a Slick Armour property rune.

The next room revealed considerably darker evidence of the activities once carried out beneath the Gauntlight. At its centre stood a large metal table fitted with leather restraints, alongside a disturbing apparatus combining a spyglass-like scope with a mechanical drill and a large black gemstone. Laurah recognised it as a device designed to assist in the casting of the Create Undead ritual, the drill apparently intended to penetrate the skull of a restrained victim while the apparatus focused necromantic energies upon them. The party debated dismantling the machine, but the discovery of its valuable black onyx component — and Erzsebet's obvious interest in the necromantic possibilities — left its fate unresolved. Their attention was then diverted by what appeared to be a giant piece of desiccated meat lying in the corner. Bonk’s investigation awakened it, revealing the "jerky" to be a gigantic undead Bloodsiphon, a leech-like horror which immediately attacked.

The resulting battle proved considerably more dangerous than the party’s earlier encounters. Erzsebet’s summoned blood thralls proved of limited effectiveness, but her recollections eventually established that the creature was resistant to slashing damage and would unleash a dangerous burst of necromantic energy when killed. After Bonk and the others hacked the creature to pieces, a final Frostbite spell from Quintus critically weakened it and triggered its death burst. The party scrambled for cover as the blood siphon exploded, dealing void damage to those caught nearby, but all survived the blast.

With the creature finally dead, they were left with the more mundane question of what to do with the necromantic machinery. The black onyx gem within the Create Undead apparatus became the subject of considerable debate. Removing it would render the device useless and provide valuable loot, but would also destroy a potentially useful tool for learning or performing necromantic rituals. Laurah was strongly committed to its destruction, while Erzsebet tearfully begged her to reconsider, she remained completely unmoved, demanding in the name of Desna that the party destroy the evil device. Nin and Bonk both agreed, motivated by the valuable gemstone.

The party then continued deeper into the workshop complex, using the ornate key they had recovered earlier to open a locked chamber. Inside they found seven alcoves containing strange mechanical and alchemical apparatus, including a large glass tank containing the preserved corpse of a goblin dressed in long-outdated servant’s clothing. Their investigations established that the workshop had been devoted to the construction of constructs and necromantic experimentation, providing further confirmation of Volluk Azrinae’s activities. The party eventually discovered that the preserved goblin could be connected to the unfinished soulbound doll they had previously encountered, and experimented by placing Mr Beak’s soul gem into the doll. This awakened the goblin’s spirit, and Erzsebet managed to calm his sobbing and converse with him.

He identified himself as Borbo and revealed that he had been trapped here for roughly five hundred years, deceived by "master" Volluk. The party attempted to question him about his former master, who he told them was a "powerful mastermind drow occultist artist craftsman” who had a relationship with a “book lady down below” and who worked for “Mistress Belcorra, who is very frightening and absolutely none of my business.” He confirmed that Volluk was prideful and vain, prone to fly into an unthinking rage when presented with images of himself before an unspecified "transformation". Erzsebet offered to take Borbo with her as part of her doll collection, but Borbo was understandably hostile to the prospect of remaining bound to another necromancer after centuries of captivity. When the party suggested that he might accompany them on their adventures instead, he remained distinctly unenthusiastic, and they ultimately decided that keeping him against his will was no better than the fate from which they had just rescued him. Rather than sell the soul gem or take it to Vandy for an exorcism, they chose to release Borbo’s soul. Bonk smashed the gem with the pommel of his sword, freeing Borbo and earning the party a supernatural premonition connecting them to Wrin, a vision of a Harrow card being drawn from a deck.

With Borbo finally liberated, the party discovered that the workshop also contained a secret passage leading down to a deeper level of the Gauntlight. The passage provided a private route from Volluk’s workshop into the main body of the complex, emerging near the flooded cavern and boathouse they had previously observed from above. Having spent approximately an hour and ten minutes exploring the lower level, including healing and dealing with their discoveries, the party now had another route into the depths available to them, alongside the unexplored areas beneath the boathouse.

Having cleared the accessible rooms, the party proceeded deeper through the interconnected passages beneath the island. They discovered that the subterranean layout corresponded closely with the geography above: the cabin beneath the boathouse was connected to the same network, while multiple routes continued towards the lower levels of the Gauntlight. A short exploration of the cavern beneath the boathouse revealed a muddy underground beach covered in mould and hanging roots, with the unpleasant coldness of some of the apparent roots suggesting that not everything in the cavern was botanical. Bonk was wounded in an attempt to clear the way through the roots, and the use of fire only made things worse - the growth was a pyrophillic Brown Mold that expanded with the heat. Quintus eventually determined that prolonged application of cold spells would wither the creature, and the party was able to proceed.

The party wisely avoided swimming through the ten-foot-deep water or attempting to investigate the tempting treasure chest positioned beyond the shore. Instead, they worked their way around the cavern, confirming that the cabin they had seen from above was indeed accessible from this lower route. The boat moored at the boathouse also bore a carved flying-bird emblem identifying it with the Osprey Club, Yinyasmera’s organisation, strengthening the party’s suspicion that it was connected to the missing men she had previously mentioned. Their caution proved justified when they reached another section of the cavern and discovered that something was waiting in the water. A thrown piece of chalk disturbed the surface, provoking a serpentine creature to rise and demand to know who dared enter the realm of Freznelkesh!

Freznelkesh proved a formidable opponent, shrugging off several of the party’s initial magical attacks and demonstrating both considerable resilience and a withering ball lightning breath that revealed her to be a Sea-Drake. The battle quickly became a test of attrition against an enemy that appeared capable of dealing enormous damage while remaining remarkably resistant to the party’s spells.

Recognising that Freznelkesh was simply too dangerous to defeat in a straight fight, the party began a fighting retreat towards the secret passage they had discovered earlier. Bonk was almost at the doorway when the sea drake came surging after him, close enough that there was no time to spare. He threw himself through the opening with Freznelkesh immediately behind him, and the party slammed the secret door shut just as the enraged drake was preparing to unleash her breath weapon once more. For a moment there was only the sound of the party's desperate breathing and the muffled fury of the creature on the other side. They had not defeated the swamp-dragon of the Fogfen, but they had escaped her alive.

The group retreated to the excavation works after lying low in Volluk's workshop for several hours, wary that Freznelkesh would pursue them on the surface. Having rendezvoused with Genevieve and the workmen, they took cover as Freznelkesh emerged into the late evening with a great shriek, barreling out of the cavern and seeking easier pretty deeper in the Fogfen. Volluk's secret passage into the subterranean servant's quarters of Gauntlight keep remains open, as does the secret door the party discovered adjacent to the chapel to Nhimbaloth on the surface. This second level of the Gauntlight complex remains almost completely unexplored, and Freznelkesh still jealously guards her hoard.
