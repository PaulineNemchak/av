Lilith bid the party farewell at the crossroads to Otari , her accumulated wounds having dissuaded her from adventuring, and returned to the fishing camp to await the arrival of her family.

The party called at Wrin’s Wonders, interrupting a card game between Wrin and another elven woman, who introduced herself as Erzsebet, a young student of occult magic who had been lodging with Wrin. Wrin responded with growing concern as the party explained their suspicions about the Gauntlight, while expressing excitement at the possibility of the party uncovering more magical artefacts from the deeper levels of the complex that they had discovered.

Erzsebet was excited by the prospect of the Island to the north of the Gauntlight that the party had yet to explore, explaining that she had read with interest the works of an obscure scholar named Volluk Azrinae, a specialist in soulbinding who was active in the region at the same time as Belcorra, and who had mentioned operating a workshop out of an island within the Fogfen in his writings. She volunteered to enlist with the party on their next expedition.

The party called on Carman Rajani to sell some of their aquired weaponry, this time at standard market rates, and Erzsebet charmed him into being both better disposed towards the party as well as giving some of his story in the town. He clearly holds a grudge against the other great families of Otari as the only descendant of the original Roseguard not to hold a position of prominence in town, and talked of his frustration that the Rosa Argentis, the Rajani family sword, was currently held by the Mehnemes family in their house museum - he had to pawn the blade to pay the mortgage on his business and has never been able to buy it back.

The party also called on the Dawnflower Library to warn Vandy Banderdash about the prospects of necromantic activity and to purchase some items to support them in fighting the undead. Vandy was unconcerned about the graveyards in Otari, which are well maintained and warded, but she did promise to provide healing and cursebreaking at a fair price to the party, and opened up the temple’s store of combat equipment, used to outfit the region’s paladins, to the party.

Laurah led introductions to the final stop on their tour of Otari, as the party checked in with Tamily at the Otari fishery. They traded tales of their adventures and Tamily joked about the new faces in the party as well as sharing when prompted her stories of encounters with sea and river drakes as a Grey Corsair. The party strategised for any future encounter with the "Swamp Dragon", and Tamily seemed excited at the prospect of another dragon head on the fishery gate, which is becoming something of a town landmark.

The following morning the party set out again for the Gauntlight to explore the island north of the keep, setting up their hirelings with an alarm snare to alert them if they were attacked while they began their excavations. While doing so they noticed a cavern beneath the boathouse, but gave that area a wide berth for now. On breaking into the small building on the Island they encountered a poor impression of a will-o-wisp, which quickly revealed itself to be a brownie, Tangletop, who had been merrily destroying a small library. He promised the party "secrets three" if they were to recover "the shiny" from a "beaky doll" in the adjoining room. The party took him up on this offer and proceeded through a hall of portraits, including a painting of Belcorra with the graffitied caption "I serve you still! You shall be avenged!" etched into the adjacent stone wall in Aklo, which Genevieve deciphered. Also represented was an image of the city of Absalom being destroyed by rising undead, and a painting of the Gauntlight lighthouse shining a beam filled with tortured spirits onto the landscape. A final portrait, titled "The Artist at Work", had been completely eviscerated.

Entering the room where they expected to find the "shiny", the party immediately noticed a spyglass of extraordinary craftsmanship on a plinth, in a room full of shattered lenswork that had clearly been destroyed in a great rage. Mr Beak — as suspected a soulbound doll — attacked Bonk immediately with a withering _vampiric touch_.

After defeating Mr Beak, the party retrieved both the spyglass and the soul gem animating the soulbound doll, intrigued by his dying words - "why do you torture poor Borbo so!". They debated keeping the clearly extremely valuable spyglass but at length determined to fulfill their bargain with Tangletop, who told them first, this building used to belong to someone named Volluk Azrinae. Second, Volluk was an apprentice to someone else named Belcorra, and even though this Volluk person seemed like someone who was mean and dangerous, Belcorra seemed much worse! Finally, the spooky magic lighthouse can do more than just shine an eerie light; it can also shoot a beam full of ghosts! These were all things the party had already theorised from the evidence, but they obtained additional information when Tangletop performed an object reading on the spyglass - Volluk Azrinae had done something terrible to himself in pursuit of power, and coundn’t bear to see his reflection. He had destroyed his protrait and smashed every reflective object in his workshop, but could not bear to damage his masterpiece, the spyglass. On retrieving the "tasty, tasty memories" in the "shiny", Tangletop disappeared in a puff of smoke.

The party then cleared the rest of the outbuilding, discovering an ornate silver key, as well as another route down to the lower levels of the Gauntlight Keep. Under the terms of their contract with Tamily's men, they must remain at the Gauntlight for the next two days - as yet, the boathouse and its cavern are yet unexplored, and the party has two routes into the lower levels available to them.
