Quintus took Olli as his familiar. We've met the ghost, Blue Finley, and he was friendly and told us about the death of his grandmother. He gave us a cold iron weapon and asked not to sell it.

A group of giant wasps had made a nest in the cliff alongside the camp, feeding on the resident spiders. They appeared friendly and should deter visitors to the camp, and provided Lilith a vial of some of their venom.

We've met Nerala, a centaur mailcoach guard with Gallentine deliveries, she said that we can regularly ride the running boards on the morning express mail coach into otari, as the camp is the last spot to water horses for free before hitting the ring of farms around Otari. She asked us to give her regards to Oloria Gallentine, the owner.

We returned the family silver to Tamily Tanderveil, she provided us a free handcart in return.

Quintus researched the Gauntlight at the Dawnflower library and uncovered the written history of Belcorra Haruvex, Otari Ilvashti and the Roseguard. Lilith caught the trail of Gorul Vetters, Shad Nunder, and Ziskilly Theed - she traced their purchase of a boat and deduced that they'd taken it to the lake on the fogfen. Nin and Bonk paid another visit to Carman at Blades for Glades to give him a piece of their minds, and the party sold all unwanted gear.

At the Gauntlight the party confronted two bands of mitflits - the first convinced Quintus to daub his face in mitflit dung. The party then stumbled into an ambush set by the mitflits luring a Slurk to attack them, before confronting another group training a giant maggot as a riding beast, The mitflits activities are being co-ordinated by a 'Boss Skrawng', who the party are preparing to confront.
